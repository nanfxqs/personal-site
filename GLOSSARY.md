# Personal Site & Exam Knowledge Base

A personal website combining a personal profile and chronological journal with a structured, cross-referenced postgraduate entrance exam (考研) knowledge base.

## Language

### Knowledge Hierarchy

**Subject (学科)**:
A top-level exam discipline in the knowledge base (408, 数学一, 英语一).
_Avoid_: 科目, 模块, 高数 (when referring to 数学一 as a whole)

**Course (书目)**:
A distinct sub-discipline or book track within a Subject (e.g., 数据结构, 计算机组成原理, 操作系统, 计算机网络 under 408; 高等数学, 线性代数, 概率论与数理统计 under 数学一).
_Avoid_: 学科, 模块

**Chapter (章节)**:
A thematic grouping of knowledge within a Course, ordered by learning logic rather than strictly bound to textbook sequence.

**Section (小节)**:
A syllabus subdivision within a Chapter that groups related Topics under a recognizable outline heading.

**Topic (知识点)**:
An atomic, self-contained knowledge unit presented as a single page, belonging to a Section and tracking its own Study Status.
_Avoid_: 文章, 笔记, 小节

**Stub Topic (待学占位节点)**:
A Topic registered in the syllabus outline whose content has not yet been written, allowing cross-references to target it without breaking navigation.
_Avoid_: 空页面, 死链

**Study Status (学习状态)**:
The learner's current mastery stage for a Topic: 未开始 (not started), 学习中 (in progress), or 已掌握 (mastered).

### Knowledge Network & Authoring

**Cross-Reference (跨科引用)**:
A directional link from one Topic (or Journal Entry) to any other Topic across any Course or Subject, which automatically surfaces as a reverse link on the target Topic.
_Avoid_: 超链接

**Reference Source (参考源)**:
An external learning material (such as a textbook section, Hello 算法, or Protocol Explorer) cited as source inspiration when authoring a Topic.
_Avoid_: 参考文献 (too narrow)

### Personal Space

**Journal Entry (个人日常)**:
A chronological personal post containing essays, thoughts, images, or audio/video, kept separate from the structured exam knowledge base with optional light references to Topics or study progress.
_Avoid_: 知识点, 笔记, 博客文章
